I feel like in today's day and age, we forget that we're human. Not in the actual physical sense, but in the meta-mental sense. But these are THE important questions we as a species, society, or however else you want to group us feel like we *should* understand but **don't**.

But is spending time worrying about the answer really time well spent? I argue that it doesn't. Anxiety is a normal emotion sure, but it's what I would categorise as '*useless*' as it typically hinders progress and action.
