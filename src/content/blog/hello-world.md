# Hello World

Hi there, I'm Mitchell Roemling, and you're (hopefully) a human reading through this. I designed and built this website in a Windows 95/98 / Vista style, because while a staple, classic, cliché or whatever else the style can be referred to for personal webpages. It was an interesting challenge to implement and learn [Astro](https://astro.build/), and it's other implementations.

# Bit About Me

While I don't want to overshare, this website has come more out of a need to share and express opinions in my own way.

### The Things I **Love**:

- Coding
- Web Design
- Support Businesses
- Self-philosophy
- New technologies
- Sketching
- [Sonny Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonny_Boy_\(TV_series\))

### The Things I Dislike:

- Social Media
- The way new technologies are being used / launched
- The lack of diversity & innovation in the Australian economy

## So What Am I Doing About It All?

I **Aspire 2** generate multiple brands and businesses that target the key issues I see in the world. I'm self driven, stubborn, and stupid enough to charge in head first through them all. I believe that ***to change society you have to start with the individual*** and ***before I can start with other individual, I first have to change myself***.

In between working the regular 9-5 (4 days a week), studying my Bachelor of Information Systems @ QUT, and my regular life, I squeeze in as much work as I can on my side-projects, research, and other growth projects.

### The End Goal?

The end-goal for me is for me to look back on my work and feel like ***in some way I helped people***. That my country, my community, and those close to me were better off for interacting with me. I don't exactly want to be immortalized in name but from my actions and I truly believe (and hope) that I'm on that path.
